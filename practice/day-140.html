Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 39.1

    Align decimal points; 30.8 + 8.3 = 39.1.

  2. 2 × 2 = 4

    The exponent counts 2 factors of 2; multiply all factors.

  3. 1:2

    Keep red first; divide 2:4 by 2.

  4. -49 m; 0 means at sea level

    Sea level is the reference. Below is negative and above is positive.

  5. Terms 4x, 5y, 4; coefficients 4, 5; constant 4

    Terms are separated by addition; coefficients multiply variables.

  6. A, C - How does height vary among the seedlings?; What is the height in centimeters of each of the seedlings?

    Different seedlings can have different height. The questions anticipate variation across a collection. The conversion and arithmetic questions have fixed answers.

  7. 43

    30 ÷ 7: write 4, subtract 28, remainder 2; bring down the next digit: 21 ÷ 7: write 3, subtract 21, remainder 0. Check: 7 × 43 = 301.

  8. x = 5

    Subtract 4 on both sides. Then x = 5; substitution gives 9 on each side.

  9. A - 183/50

    Align decimal points; 6.76 - 3.1 = 3.66.

  10. C, D - (12, 10); (18, 15)

    Equivalent ratios multiply both quantities by the same factor. A uses 2; B uses 3. C and D change only one scaled quantity.

  11. 61

    Parentheses: 6 - 3 = 3. Exponent: 7² = 49. Multiply: 4 × 3 = 12. Add: 49 + 12 = 61. The product must be completed before the final sum.

  12. C - 10062/125

    Compute the whole-number product and restore decimal places: 9.36 × 8.6 = 80.496.

  13. C, D - (6 × 4) ÷ 2; 3 × 4

    Triangle area is half the matching parallelogram: (6 × 4)/2 = 12 cm². Multiplying half the base by the height gives the same result.

  14. 7x + 6y - 3

    Marker cost is 7x; folder cost is 6y. Add these subtotals, then subtract the one fixed coupon of 3. An expression does not need an equals sign.

  15. A - 33/5

    Multiply both numbers by 10: 158.4 ÷ 24 = 6.6. Check: 2.4 × 6.6 = 15.84.

  16. Aligned pairs (0,0), (7,3), (14,6), (35,15); 15 m²

    Each aligned jump adds 7 liters and 3 m². Both quantities scale by 5; the number lines measure different units.

  17. 13

    Substitute: 6 × (3/2) + 2². The product is 9 and the square is 4. Add to get 13.

  18. D - 1

    Each small part is 1/10; 5 shaded parts divided into groups of 5 gives 5/5 = 1 groups.

  19. B, C - 4 notebooks cost $12.; Each notebook costs $3.

    Cost per notebook = 18 ÷ 6 = 3. Multiply $3 by the chosen notebook count.

  20. C, D - 3x + 18; 18 + 3x

    Distribute 3 to both terms to get 3x + 18. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

  21. B - 1/5

    1/2 ÷ 5/2 = 1/2 × 2/5 = 1/5.

  22. Removed 3 m²; remaining 27 m²

    Outer area = 30. Triangle = 3 × 2/2 = 3. Subtract the removed region exactly once.

  23. B, D - 8x + 3; (4 + 4)x + 3

    Combine coefficients of like x terms: 4 + 4 = 8. The constant 3 stays separate.

  24. B - 32/19

    4 ÷ 19/8 = 4 × 8/19 = 32/19.

  25. A: $7 each; B: $8 each; A is the better buy.

    Divide each cost by its own notebook count: 35/5 = 7; 48/6 = 8. Compare the same one-notebook unit.

  26. B, D - 15 + 5x; 5x + 15

    Distribute 5 to both terms to get 5x + 15. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

  27. A, B - 15 is the largest number of identical no-leftover kits from 30 pencils and 45 erasers.; 15 divides both 30 and 45.

    GCF(30, 45) = 15. Divide each count by 15: 2 pencils and 3 erasers per kit. A larger shared divisor is impossible.

  28. (6, 12, 15, 18, 24); IQR 6 min

    Minimum and maximum anchor whiskers. Box ends are 12, 18; median line is 15. IQR = 18 - 12.

  29. x = 10

    Add 9 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 10 = x. Substitute 10 for x in x − 9 to check that the right side equals 1.

  30. A, C - In 2 hours it travels 30 km.; The speed is 15 km per hour.

    Divide distance by time: 45 ÷ 3 = 15 km/h. Multiply this unit rate by 2 for the later distance.

  31. A, B - -5 lies to the right of -7 on a number line.; -7 < -5

    Among negatives, the number closer to zero is greater. Thus -5 is warmer and is farther right.

  32. B, C - 14/4; 7/2

    Use the inverse operation on both sides. The solution is 7/2; the doubled numerator and denominator name the same value. Substitution verifies the solution.

  33. C, D - 15/2 × 12 inches; 90 inches

    There are 12 smaller units in each larger one. Multiply 15/2 by 12, giving 90 inches. The physical length does not change.

  34. A, C - -6 is farther from zero than 4.; |-6| = 6

    Absolute value measures distance: 6 versus 4. A larger distance does not make the negative number greater.

  35. x = 18

    Multiply by 3 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 18 = x. Substitute 18 for x in x ÷ 3 to check that the right side equals 6.

  36. 20 participants; n = (25/100) × 80; less than the whole because the percent is below 100

    Rename 25% as 1/4. Multiply by 80 to obtain 20. Compare the rate with 1.

  37. 108 cm²; open box 84 cm²

    Closed area = 2(24+18+12) = 108. Subtract exactly one top face, 24, for the open box.

  38. 9 observations; highest intervals 5–9

    Use four equal-width touching bars with heights 2, 4, 1, 2. Frequencies sum to 9. Include every interval with frequency 4.

  39. 0 bottles; 3/5 L left

    3/5 ÷ 1 = 3/5 × 1 = 3/5. Only whole bottles count; subtract the liquid used.

  40. B, C - 8; 7

    Subtract 6 conceptually: x must exceed 6. Equality at 6 fails because the inequality is strict.

  41. A, D - (30/100)w = 24; w = 24 ÷ (30/100)

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 24 by 3/10; w = 80. Check by multiplication.

  42. P: II; Q: IV; R is on the y-axis.

    P has signs (-,+), so II. Q has (+,-), so IV. R has x = 0 and is on an axis.

  43. 30 ≤ x; closed circle at 30, shade right

    Start with x ≥ 30. Swap the two sides and reverse the comparison sign: 30 ≤ x. Use a closed circle at 30 and shade right.

  44. 165/8 m³; 165 cubes

    Multiply edges: 11/2 × 5/2 × 3/2 = 165/8. The edge counts are 11, 5, 3, giving 165 cubes. Each cube is 1/8 m³; 165/8 = 165/8.

  45. 75%; denominator 60

    Percent = (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%. The denominator is the reference whole, not automatically the larger number.

  46. AB = 9 units; AC = 12 units

    AB is horizontal: 5 + 4 = 9. AC is vertical: 6 + 6 = 12. Each segment crosses an axis.

  47. (0,0), (1,6), (6,36); independent h; L = 6h

    Hours are the input. Multiply each hour value by 6 to obtain liters. Graph hours horizontally and liters vertically.

  48. 6 observations; minutes; median 6, IQR 2; mean 22/3, MAD 29/9. A high value makes median/IQR a useful choice.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 6 and half medians give IQR 2. Mean is 44/6; averaging distances gives MAD 29/9. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.
