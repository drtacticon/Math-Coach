Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 3:1

    Keep red first; divide 6:2 by 2.

  2. 6; 18

    Multiply both parts by 2 and by 6.

  3. Mix B

    For 14 cups water, A uses 4 cups juice; B uses 6. B has more juice per equal water amount.

  4. 35 oaks and 20 pines

    11 equal sections total; each section is 55 ÷ 11 = 5.

  5. 1/5; 0.2

    20% = 20/100 = 1/5; divide by 100 for 0.2.

  6. A, B - 75/100; 0.75

    Percent means per 100. Divide 75 by 100 to get 0.75; simplify 75/100 to 3/4.

  7. 8/5; 1.6

    160% = 160/100 = 8/5. Dividing by 100 gives 1.6, which exceeds 1 because 160 exceeds 100.

  8. 72

    30/100 × 240 = 72.

  9. 2/5; 0.4

    40% = 40/100 = 2/5; divide by 100 for 0.4.

  10. 24

    60/100 × 40 = 24.

  11. 4

    5/100 × 80 = 4.

  12. A, D - w = 15 ÷ (15/100); (15/100)w = 15

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 15 by 3/20; w = 100. Check by multiplication.

  13. C, D - w = 12 ÷ (20/100); (20/100)w = 12

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 12 by 1/5; w = 60. Check by multiplication.

  14. A, C - (25/100)w = 10; w = 10 ÷ (25/100)

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 10 by 1/4; w = 40. Check by multiplication.

  15. 60%; denominator 60

    Percent = (36 ÷ 60) × 100 = 60%. The denominator is the reference whole, not automatically the larger number.

  16. A, D - w = 50 ÷ (125/100); (125/100)w = 50

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 50 by 5/4; w = 40. Check by multiplication.

  17. A, D - (20/100)w = 16; w = 16 ÷ (20/100)

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 16 by 1/5; w = 80. Check by multiplication.

  18. B, D - (125/100)w = 150; w = 150 ÷ (125/100)

    The part is percent rate times whole. Divide 150 by 5/4; w = 120. Check by multiplication.

  19. A, D - 325/100; 3.25

    Percent means per 100. Divide 325 by 100 to get 3.25; simplify 325/100 to 13/4.

  20. B, D - 375/100; 3.75

    Percent means per 100. Divide 375 by 100 to get 3.75; simplify 375/100 to 15/4.
