Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. No; left side = 46

    7 × 6 + 4 = 46; compare with 39.

  2. x = 4

    Subtract 3 on both sides. Then x = 4; substitution gives 7 on each side.

  3. x = 9

    Add 9 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 9 = x. Substitute 9 for x in x − 9 to check that the right side equals 0.

  4. x = 4

    Divide by 4 on both sides. Then x = 4; substitution gives 16 on each side.

  5. 4:1

    Keep red first; divide 8:2 by 2.

  6. 14; 28

    Multiply both parts by 2 and by 4.

  7. Mix B

    For 15 cups water, A uses 15 cups juice; B uses 19. B has more juice per equal water amount.

  8. 12 oaks and 30 pines

    7 equal sections total; each section is 42 ÷ 7 = 6.

  9. 6; 9

    Multiply both parts by 2 and by 3.

  10. 20 square meters; aligned pairs (0,0), (7,5), (28,20)

    28 ÷ 7 = 4 batches; 4 × 5 = 20.

  11. (0,0), (8,5), (16,10), (24,15); 24 tickets earns $15

    Scale both quantities by each batch count; plot horizontal tickets and vertical dollars.

  12. C, D - Each notebook costs $4.; 4 notebooks cost $16.

    Cost per notebook = 12 ÷ 3 = 4. Multiply $4 by the chosen notebook count.

  13. A: $5 each; B: $6 each; A is the better buy.

    Divide each cost by its own notebook count: 10/2 = 5; 18/3 = 6. Compare the same one-notebook unit.

  14. A, C - 3 notebooks cost $18.; Each notebook costs $6.

    Cost per notebook = 12 ÷ 2 = 6. Multiply $6 by the chosen notebook count.

  15. B, C - The speed is 25 km per hour.; In 3 hours it travels 75 km.

    Divide distance by time: 125 ÷ 5 = 25 km/h. Multiply this unit rate by 3 for the later distance.

  16. A, C - 78 inches; 13/2 × 12 inches

    There are 12 smaller units in each larger one. Multiply 13/2 by 12, giving 78 inches. The physical length does not change.

  17. C, D - 7/2 × 100 centimeters; 350 centimeters

    There are 100 smaller units in each larger one. Multiply 7/2 by 100, giving 350 centimeters. The physical length does not change.

  18. A, C - The speed is 15 km per hour.; In 3 hours it travels 45 km.

    Divide distance by time: 60 ÷ 4 = 15 km/h. Multiply this unit rate by 3 for the later distance.

  19. B, C - The fraction of all tiles that are red is 1/2.; The red-to-blue ratio is 3:3.

    Divide both color counts by 3. For the fraction of the whole, use total 18 as denominator; red-to-blue compares just the two colors.

  20. C, D - (12, 6); (18, 9)

    Equivalent ratios multiply both quantities by the same factor. A uses 2; B uses 3. C and D change only one scaled quantity.
