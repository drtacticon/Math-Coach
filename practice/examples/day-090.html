Example for question 3

Solve x - 3/2 = 2. Select every representation of the solution.

  • A. 9/2
  • B. 11/2
  • C. 14/4
  • D. 7/2
  1. Use the inverse operation on both sides.
  2. The solution is 7/2
  3. the doubled numerator and denominator name the same value.
  4. Substitution verifies the solution.

Answer: C, D - 14/4; 7/2

Example for question 13

Store A sells 3 notebooks for $18. Store B sells 4 notebooks for $28. Part A: enter the unit price at each store. Part B: choose the better buy and explain why package totals alone are insufficient.

    1. Divide each cost by its own notebook count: 18/3 = 6
    2. 28/4 = 7.
    3. Compare the same one-notebook unit.

    Answer: A: $6 each; B: $7 each; A is the better buy.

    Example for question 15

    A bicycle travels 105 km in 3 hours at constant speed. Select all true statements.

    • A. In 1 hour it travels 36 km.
    • B. The speed is 35 km per hour.
    • C. In 2 hours it travels 70 km.
    • D. The speed is 108 km per hour.
    1. Divide distance by time: 105 ÷ 3 = 35 km/h.
    2. Multiply this unit rate by 2 for the later distance.

    Answer: B, C - The speed is 35 km per hour.; In 2 hours it travels 70 km.
