      Example for question 25

      Store A sells 6 notebooks for $36. Store B sells 7 notebooks for $49. Part A: enter the unit price at each store. Part B: choose the better buy and explain why package totals alone are insufficient.

        1. Divide each cost by its own notebook count: 36/6 = 6
        2. 49/7 = 7.
        3. Compare the same one-notebook unit.

        Answer: A: $6 each; B: $7 each; A is the better buy.

        Example for question 26

        Select all expressions equivalent to 7(x + 4).

        • A. 11x
        • B. 7x + 28
        • C. 7x + 4
        • D. 28 + 7x
        1. Distribute 7 to both terms to get 7x + 28.
        2. Reversing the addends gives the same value.
        3. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

        Answer: B, D - 7x + 28; 28 + 7x

        Example for question 30

        A bicycle travels 30 km in 3 hours at constant speed. Select all true statements.

        • A. In 4 hours it travels 40 km.
        • B. The speed is 33 km per hour.
        • C. In 1 hour it travels 11 km.
        • D. The speed is 10 km per hour.
        1. Divide distance by time: 30 ÷ 3 = 10 km/h.
        2. Multiply this unit rate by 4 for the later distance.

        Answer: A, D - In 4 hours it travels 40 km.; The speed is 10 km per hour.

        Example for question 31

        Temperatures are -8 °C and -6 °C. Select all true comparisons.

        • A. -8 is warmer than -6.
        • B. -6 lies to the right of -8 on a number line.
        • C. -8 < -6
        • D. -8 > 0
        1. Among negatives, the number closer to zero is greater.
        2. Thus -6 is warmer and is farther right.

        Answer: B, C - -6 lies to the right of -8 on a number line.; -8 < -6

          Example for question 41

          12 is 30% of an unknown quantity w. Select all equations that find w.

          • A. (30/100)w = 12
          • B. w = 12 ÷ (30/100)
          • C. w = 12 × (30/100)
          • D. w = 12 + 30
          1. The part is percent rate times whole.
          2. Divide 12 by 3/10
          3. w = 40.
          4. Check by multiplication.

          Answer: A, B - (30/100)w = 12; w = 12 ÷ (30/100)
