Example for question 7

Solve x - 1 = 7/2. Select every representation of the solution.

  • A. 9/2
  • B. 13/2
  • C. 11/2
  • D. 18/4
  1. Use the inverse operation on both sides.
  2. The solution is 9/2
  3. the doubled numerator and denominator name the same value.
  4. Substitution verifies the solution.

Answer: A, D - 9/2; 18/4
