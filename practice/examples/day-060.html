Example for question 10

Select all expressions equal to 35 + 28.

  • A. 35 + 28
  • B. 7(5 + 4)
  • C. 7 × 10
  • D. 64
  1. Distribute 7 to both terms: 7 × 5 + 7 × 4 = 63.
  2. The other choices are 7 or 1 too large.

Answer: A, B - 35 + 28; 7(5 + 4)

Example for question 13

Let x = 1 and y = 5. Evaluate 5x + y². Part A: enter the value. Part B: show substitution and the order of operations.

    1. Substitute: 5 × (1) + 5².
    2. The product is 5 and the square is 25.
    3. Add to get 30.

    Answer: 30
