Example for question 12

20 is 25% of an unknown quantity w. Select all equations that find w.

  • A. w = 20 + 25
  • B. w = 20 ÷ (25/100)
  • C. w = 20 × (25/100)
  • D. (25/100)w = 20
  1. The part is percent rate times whole.
  2. Divide 20 by 1/4
  3. w = 80.
  4. Check by multiplication.

Answer: B, D - w = 20 ÷ (25/100); (25/100)w = 20
