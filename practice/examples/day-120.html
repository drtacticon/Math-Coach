Worked examples

Example for question 7

A 8 m by 6 m rectangle has a right-triangle corner removed with perpendicular legs 4 m and 2 m. Part A: enter the removed area. Part B: enter the remaining area and justify subtraction.

    1. Outer area = 48.
    2. Triangle = 4 × 2/2 = 4.
    3. Subtract the removed region exactly once.

    Answer: Removed 4 m²; remaining 44 m²

    Example for question 11

    A closed box is 4 cm by 3 cm by 4 cm. Part A: enter its surface area. Part B: the 4-by-3 top is removed; enter the remaining surface area and explain.

      1. Closed area = 2(12+16+12) = 80.
      2. Subtract exactly one top face, 12, for the open box.

      Answer: 80 cm²; open box 68 cm²
