Worked examples

Example for question 3

A 11 m by 11 m rectangle has a right-triangle corner removed with perpendicular legs 5 m and 6 m. Part A: enter the removed area. Part B: enter the remaining area and justify subtraction.

    1. Outer area = 121.
    2. Triangle = 5 × 6/2 = 15.
    3. Subtract the removed region exactly once.

    Answer: Removed 15 m²; remaining 106 m²

    Example for question 6

    Six students recorded reading times (minutes): 4, 6, 6, 8, 8, 10. Part A: enter the mean. Part B: a seventh student reads 12 minutes. Enter the new mean and explain the denominator.

      1. Original sum 42 divided by 6 gives 7.
      2. Add 12, then divide the new sum by 7 because there are now 7 observations.

      Answer: 7 min; new mean 54/7 min

      Example for question 7

      A frequency table records books read: 0 books: 5 students; 1: 1; 2: 2; 3: 3. Part A: draw a dot plot. Part B: enter the observation count and explain how to verify it.

        1. Place one dot per student, not one per distinct value.
        2. Total dots = 5 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 11.
        3. Label the number line books read.

        Answer: 11 students; stacks at 0, 1, 2, 3 have 5, 1, 2, 3 dots.
