Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. +18 m; 0 means at sea level

    Sea level is the reference. Below is negative and above is positive.

  2. 3

    -3 and 3 are each 3 units from 0 on opposite sides.

  3. -7 < 0 < 3; unit steps

    Negative -7 is left of or below 0; 3 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

  4. -2 < 0 < 4; unit steps

    Negative -2 is left of or below 0; 4 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

  5. 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16

    The exponent counts 4 factors of 2; multiply all factors.

  6. 10

    Use parentheses and exponents first, then × and ÷ left to right, then + and -. Result: 10.

  7. 4

    Common factors: 1, 2, 4; greatest is 4.

  8. 12

    The first positive multiple shared by 4 and 3 is 12.

  9. 14

    Common factors: 1, 2, 7, 14; greatest is 14.

  10. 24(2 + 3)

    GCF = 24; 48 = 24 × 2 and 72 = 24 × 3.

  11. Outer quotient; dividend (7x + 3); divisor 6; inner sum 7x + 3

    The grouped sum is one complete quantity being divided. Within it, the terms are 7x and 3.

  12. 2x + 6y - 2

    Marker cost is 2x; folder cost is 6y. Add these subtotals, then subtract the one fixed coupon of 2. An expression does not need an equals sign.

  13. 6

    Substitute: 2 × (1) + 2². The product is 2 and the square is 4. Add to get 6.

  14. B, C - 5x + 10; 10 + 5x

    Distribute 5 to both terms to get 5x + 10. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

  15. A, D - 4x + 8; 8 + 4x

    Distribute 4 to both terms to get 4x + 8. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

  16. A, C - (3 + 3)x + 2; 6x + 2

    Combine coefficients of like x terms: 3 + 3 = 6. The constant 2 stays separate.

  17. A, D - 16 + 4x; 4x + 16

    Distribute 4 to both terms to get 4x + 16. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

  18. 30

    Substitute: 7 × (2) + 4². The product is 14 and the square is 16. Add to get 30.

  19. B, D - 27; 3 × 3 × 3

    The exponent 3 means three factors of 3; their product is 27. It does not mean 3 times 3.

  20. 19

    Parentheses: 6 - 3 = 3. Exponent: 2² = 4. Multiply: 5 × 3 = 15. Add: 4 + 15 = 19. The product must be completed before the final sum.
