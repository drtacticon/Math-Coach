Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 1/8

    1/2 × 1/4 = 1/8.

  2. 7/2

    3/2 × 7/3 = 7/2.

  3. 1

    Each small part is 1/6; 4 shaded parts divided into groups of 4 gives 4/4 = 1 groups.

  4. 2

    Each small part is 1/10; 4 shaded parts divided into groups of 2 gives 4/2 = 2 groups.

  5. +20 m; 0 means at sea level

    Sea level is the reference. Below is negative and above is positive.

  6. 5

    -5 and 5 are each 5 units from 0 on opposite sides.

  7. -3 < 0 < 2; unit steps

    Negative -3 is left of or below 0; 2 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

  8. -4 < 0 < 1; use steps of 1/2

    Negative -4 is left of or below 0; 1 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

  9. -4/5 < 0 < 1; use steps of 1/5

    Negative -4/5 is left of or below 0; 1 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

  10. >

    -2/3 > -1; greater numbers lie farther right.

  11. <

    -3 < -3/2; greater numbers lie farther right.

  12. C, D - -7 is farther from zero than 5.; |-7| = 7

    Absolute value measures distance: 7 versus 5. A larger distance does not make the negative number greater.

  13. A, B - |-4| = 4; -4 is farther from zero than 2.

    Absolute value measures distance: 4 versus 2. A larger distance does not make the negative number greater.

  14. P: II; Q: IV; R is on the y-axis.

    P has signs (-,+), so II. Q has (+,-), so IV. R has x = 0 and is on an axis.

  15. P: II; Q: IV; R is on the y-axis.

    P has signs (-,+), so II. Q has (+,-), so IV. R has x = 0 and is on an axis.

  16. A, C - Across the x-axis: (-7, -3).; Across the y-axis: (7, 3).

    The x-axis reflection changes y only; the y-axis reflection changes x only. Across both gives (7, -3).

  17. AB = 9 units; AC = 6 units

    AB is horizontal: 5 + 4 = 9. AC is vertical: 3 + 3 = 6. Each segment crosses an axis.

  18. A, C - Across the y-axis: (3, 4).; Across the x-axis: (-3, -4).

    The x-axis reflection changes y only; the y-axis reflection changes x only. Across both gives (3, -4).

  19. B, C - Its signed position is -6 m.; A reading of 0 m means at the reference point.

    Below the chosen zero is negative, so the position is -6. Zero identifies the reference; the opposite is 6 m above it.

  20. A, D - The opposite of the opposite of -5/2 is -5/2.; The opposite of -5/2 is 5/2.

    An opposite reverses direction without changing distance. Reversing twice returns to -5/2. Zero is its own opposite.
