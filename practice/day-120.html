Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. B, D - 0.15; 15/100

    Percent means per 100. Divide 15 by 100 to get 0.15; simplify 15/100 to 3/20.

  2. B, D - 0.6; 60/100

    Percent means per 100. Divide 60 by 100 to get 0.6; simplify 60/100 to 3/5.

  3. 51/20; 2.55

    255% = 255/100 = 51/20. Dividing by 100 gives 2.55, which exceeds 1 because 255 exceeds 100.

  4. 60

    50/100 × 120 = 60.

  5. 4 cm²

    A = bh/2 = 4 × 2 ÷ 2 = 4. Two such triangles make a parallelogram.

  6. 10 m²

    Cut and shift a corner; A = bh = 2 × 5 = 10.

  7. 26 m²

    Outer area 54 minus cutout 28 = 26.

  8. Sides 8 and 9; area 72 square units

    Horizontal length 6 - (-2) = 8; vertical length 6 - (-3) = 9; area = 8 × 9.

  9. Sides 10 and 11; area 110 square units

    Horizontal length 5 - (-5) = 10; vertical length 5 - (-6) = 11; area = 10 × 11.

  10. 2 faces each of 6×2, 6×2, 2×2; 56 cm²

    Add 2(12) + 2(12) + 2(4) = 56.

  11. 292 cm²

    Closed surface area = 2(56+48+42) = 292.

  12. Face 35 cm²; total 240 cm²

    One face = (10 × 7)/2 = 35. Four faces total 140. Add square base area 100.

  13. 21/2 m³; 84 cubes

    Multiply edges: 7/2 × 2 × 3/2 = 21/2. The edge counts are 7, 4, 3, giving 84 cubes. Each cube is 1/8 m³; 84/8 = 21/2.

  14. 64 cm²; open box 56 cm²

    Closed area = 2(8+16+8) = 64. Subtract exactly one top face, 8, for the open box.

  15. 15/2 m³; 60 cubes

    Multiply edges: 15/2 × 1 × 1 = 15/2. The edge counts are 15, 2, 2, giving 60 cubes. Each cube is 1/8 m³; 60/8 = 15/2.

  16. 27/4 m³; 54 cubes

    Multiply edges: 9/2 × 3/2 × 1 = 27/4. The edge counts are 9, 3, 2, giving 54 cubes. Each cube is 1/8 m³; 54/8 = 27/4.

  17. 122 cm²; open box 94 cm²

    Closed area = 2(28+21+12) = 122. Subtract exactly one top face, 28, for the open box.

  18. Removed 25/2 m²; remaining 135/2 m²

    Outer area = 80. Triangle = 5 × 5/2 = 25/2. Subtract the removed region exactly once.

  19. C, D - 5 × 6; (10 × 6) ÷ 2

    Triangle area is half the matching parallelogram: (10 × 6)/2 = 30 cm². Multiplying half the base by the height gives the same result.

  20. 44 m²

    Use average base × height: (8 + 14)/2 × 4 = 44. A full rectangle at the longer base includes extra corner regions.
