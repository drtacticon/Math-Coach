Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 4 × 4 × 4 × 4 = 256

    The exponent counts 4 factors of 4; multiply all factors.

  2. 25

    Use parentheses and exponents first, then × and ÷ left to right, then + and -. Result: 25.

  3. 4

    Common factors: 1, 2, 4; greatest is 4.

  4. 6

    The first positive multiple shared by 3 and 2 is 6.

  5. No; left side = 38

    5 × 7 + 3 = 38; compare with 33.

  6. x = 10

    Subtract 8 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 10 = x. Substitute 10 for x in x + 8 to check that the right side equals 18.

  7. x = 5

    Add 4 on both sides. Then x = 5; substitution gives 1 on each side.

  8. x = 11

    Divide by 2 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 11 = x. Substitute 11 for x in 2x to check that the right side equals 22.

  9. x = 3/2

    Subtract 4 on both sides. Then x = 3/2; substitution gives 11/2 on each side.

  10. x = 40

    Multiply by 5 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 40 = x. Substitute 40 for x in x ÷ 5 to check that the right side equals 8.

  11. 6x = 48; x = 8 m

    Divide the total by 6: 48 ÷ 6 = 8.

  12. 12 ≥ x; closed circle at 12, shade left

    Start with x ≤ 12. Swap the two sides and reverse the comparison sign: 12 ≥ x. Use a closed circle at 12 and shade left.

  13. 27

    Read the inequality as x > 26. Substitute each value. The values that work lie to the right of 26. The endpoint does not work.

  14. x ≥ 13; closed circle at 13, shade right

    Start with x ≥ 13. Use a closed circle at 13 and shade right.

  15. 10

    Read the inequality as x < 11. Substitute each value. The values that work lie to the left of 11. The endpoint does not work.

  16. (0,0), (1,5), (4,20); independent h; L = 5h

    Hours are the input. Multiply each hour value by 5 to obtain liters. Graph hours horizontally and liters vertically.

  17. (0,0), (1,3), (3,9); independent h; L = 3h

    Hours are the input. Multiply each hour value by 3 to obtain liters. Graph hours horizontally and liters vertically.

  18. (0,0), (1,3), (4,12); independent h; L = 3h

    Hours are the input. Multiply each hour value by 3 to obtain liters. Graph hours horizontally and liters vertically.

  19. B, C - 3; 4

    Subtract 5 conceptually: x must exceed 2. Equality at 2 fails because the inequality is strict.

  20. x = 8

    Subtract 8 on both sides to isolate x. This gives 8 = x. Substitute 8 for x in x + 8 to check that the right side equals 16.
