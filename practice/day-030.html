Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 26.5

    Align decimal points; 17.9 + 8.6 = 26.5.

  2. 60

    Align decimal points; 63.1 - 3.1 = 60.

  3. 749.7

    Compute the whole-number product and restore decimal places: 88.2 × 8.5 = 749.7.

  4. 76

    60 ÷ 8: write 7, subtract 56, remainder 4; bring down the next digit: 48 ÷ 8: write 6, subtract 48, remainder 0. Check: 8 × 76 = 608.

  5. 1/4

    1 × 1/4 = 1/4.

  6. 25/4

    5/2 × 5/2 = 25/4.

  7. 1

    Each small part is 1/12; 2 shaded parts divided into groups of 2 gives 2/2 = 1 groups.

  8. 2

    Each small part is 1/4; 4 shaded parts divided into groups of 2 gives 4/2 = 2 groups.

  9. 2

    Each small part is 1/8; 2 shaded parts divided into groups of 1 gives 2/1 = 2 groups.

  10. 7/12

    7/6 ÷ 2 = 7/6 × 1/2 = 7/12.

  11. 16/3

    4/3 ÷ 1/4 = 4/3 × 4 = 16/3.

  12. C - 15/19

    15/8 ÷ 19/8 = 15/8 × 8/19 = 15/19.

  13. C - 5/3

    8/3 ÷ 8/5 = 8/3 × 5/8 = 5/3.

  14. C - 7/4

    7/4 ÷ 1 = 7/4 × 1 = 7/4.

  15. n = 4; 1 ÷ 1/4 = 4

    1 ÷ 1/4 = 1 × 4 = 4. Check: 1/4 × 4 = 1.

  16. 0 bottles; 1/8 L left

    1/8 ÷ 3/8 = 1/8 × 8/3 = 1/3. Only whole bottles count; subtract the liquid used.

  17. 2 bottles; 2/5 L left

    2 ÷ 4/5 = 2 × 5/4 = 5/2. Only whole bottles count; subtract the liquid used.

  18. C - 2

    2 ÷ 1 = 2 × 1 = 2.

  19. C - 1

    1 × 1 = 1.

  20. B - 9/4

    3/2 × 3/2 = 9/4.
