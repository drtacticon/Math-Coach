Practice answer key

Show answers and reasoning
  1. 12 cm²

    A = bh/2 = 8 × 3 ÷ 2 = 12. Two such triangles make a parallelogram.

  2. 30 m²

    Cut and shift a corner; A = bh = 5 × 6 = 30.

  3. 85 m²

    Outer area 120 minus cutout 35 = 85.

  4. Sides 8 and 10; area 80 square units

    Horizontal length 8 - (0) = 8; vertical length 10 - (0) = 10; area = 8 × 10.

  5. A, D - How does race time vary among the runners?; What is the race time in seconds of each of the runners?

    Different runners can have different race time. The questions anticipate variation across a collection. The conversion and arithmetic questions have fixed answers.

  6. n = 6; minutes; mean 11/2 min

    Sum = 33; mean = 33 ÷ 6 = 11/2.

  7. 3: 1 dot; 4: 2 dots; 5: 2 dots; 6: 1 dot; n = 6

    Each observation contributes one dot; stack repeated values at their number-line position.

  8. (4, 10, 13, 16, 22)

    Box runs from 10 to 16, median line at 13, whiskers to 4 and 22.

  9. 5: 1 dot; 9: 2 dots; 13: 2 dots; 17: 1 dot; n = 6

    Each observation contributes one dot; stack repeated values at their number-line position.

  10. Frequencies 2, 1, 2, 5

    Count every value once. Bars touch and have equal width; horizontal axis is minutes, vertical axis is frequency.

  11. Q1 12, Q3 16; IQR 4 min; range 12 min

    Lower half [8, 12, 12] has median 12; upper half [16, 16, 20] has median 16. Subtract Q1 from Q3.

  12. Mean 9 min; MAD 5/3 min

    Mean = 54/6 = 9. Absolute deviations: 3, 1, 1, 1, 1, 3. Their sum divided by 6 is 5/3. This is the average distance from the mean.

  13. 6 observations; minutes; median 25/2, IQR 5; mean 25/2, MAD 25/6. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 25/2 and half medians give IQR 5. Mean is 75/6; averaging distances gives MAD 25/6. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

  14. 6 observations; minutes; median 11, IQR 4; mean 11, MAD 10/3. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 11 and half medians give IQR 4. Mean is 66/6; averaging distances gives MAD 10/3. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

  15. 6 observations; minutes; median 13, IQR 4; mean 13, MAD 10/3. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 13 and half medians give IQR 4. Mean is 78/6; averaging distances gives MAD 10/3. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

  16. 6 observations; minutes; median 21/2, IQR 5; mean 21/2, MAD 25/6. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 21/2 and half medians give IQR 5. Mean is 63/6; averaging distances gives MAD 25/6. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

  17. First median 12; second 15 min; both IQR 6 min.

    Adding 3 shifts every position and both quartiles by 3. The median increases by 3; Q3 - Q1 remains 6.

  18. 12 students; stacks at 0, 1, 2, 3 have 3, 5, 3, 1 dots.

    Place one dot per student, not one per distinct value. Total dots = 3 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 12. Label the number line books read.

  19. A, C - How does mass vary among the backpacks?; What is the mass in kilograms of each of the backpacks?

    Different backpacks can have different mass. The questions anticipate variation across a collection. The conversion and arithmetic questions have fixed answers.

  20. A, D - The median is 10 minutes.; The median is the average of the 3rd and 4th ordered values.

    Six ordered observations have middle positions 3 and 4, so median = (9 + 11)/2 = 10.
