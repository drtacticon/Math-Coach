enVision Topic 3: Numeric and Algebraic Expressions | Spectrum companion Chapter 1

LEARNING TARGET

I can find the least common multiple of two numbers no greater than 12.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

List positive multiples of both numbers. The smallest shared multiple is the LCM. Repeating events that begin together next occur together after this many time units.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Find the LCM of 2 and 3 by listing multiples.

  1. List positive multiples of both numbers.
  2. The smallest shared multiple is the LCM.
  3. The first positive multiple shared by 2 and 3 is 6.
  4. State the result in the form requested: 6.
6
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple select

Two signals flash together now and then every 9 and 2 seconds. Select all true statements.

A. The next shared time is 19 seconds.

B. They next flash together after 18 seconds.

C. 36 seconds is also a time they flash together.

D. They next flash together after 1 seconds.

  1. List positive multiples of both numbers.
  2. List multiples or use prime factors.
  3. The least shared positive multiple is 18
  4. multiples of 18 are later shared times.
B, C - They next flash together after 18 seconds.; 36 seconds is also a time they flash together.
