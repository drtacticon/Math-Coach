Day 023 · Lesson 08Divide Mixed Numbers

LearnPracticeDaily PDF ↗
Standards 6.NS.A.1

enVision Topic 1: Use Positive Rational Numbers | Spectrum companion Chapter 2

LEARNING TARGET

I can divide mixed numbers after renaming them as fractions.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Rename a mixed number as (whole × denominator + numerator)/denominator. Keep the dividend, change division to multiplication, and use the reciprocal of the divisor. Simplify and interpret the result.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Calculate 2 ÷ 1/2. Simplify.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Write each number as a fraction. Convert mixed numbers before calculating.

    2/1 ÷ 1/2
  2. Step 2

    Keep the first fraction. Change division to multiplication and flip the second fraction.

    2 × 2
  3. Step 3

    Multiply the numerators. Then multiply the denominators.

    (2 × 2) / (1 × 1) = 4/1
  4. Step 4

    Simplify the fraction.

    4
  5. Step 5

    Check by multiplying the quotient by the original divisor.

    4 × 1/2 = 2
Answer: 4
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple choice

Calculate 3 1/3 ÷ 4 1/2. Simplify and explain why your answer is reasonable. Select one answer, then justify it with a model or calculation.

A. -7/27

B. 20/27

C. 47/27

D. 27/20

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Write each number as a fraction. Convert mixed numbers before calculating.

    10/3 ÷ 9/2
  2. Step 2

    Keep the first fraction. Change division to multiplication and flip the second fraction.

    10/3 × 2/9
  3. Step 3

    Multiply the numerators. Then multiply the denominators.

    (10 × 2) / (3 × 9) = 20/27
  4. Step 4

    Simplify the fraction.

    20/27
  5. Step 5

    Check by multiplying the quotient by the original divisor.

    20/27 × 9/2 = 10/3
Answer: B - 20/27
