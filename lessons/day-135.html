Unit Test

  2. Previous unit review · Constructed response

  3. Previous unit review · Constructed response

  4. Previous unit review · Constructed response

03 · CHECK

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
  1. 16 cm²

    A = bh/2 = 8 × 4 ÷ 2 = 16. Two such triangles make a parallelogram.

    6.G.A.1
  2. 6 m²

    Cut and shift a corner; A = bh = 2 × 3 = 6.

    6.G.A.1
  3. 32 m²

    Outer area 36 minus cutout 4 = 32.

    6.G.A.1
  4. Sides 9 and 9; area 81 square units

    Horizontal length 9 - (0) = 9; vertical length 9 - (0) = 9; area = 9 × 9.

    6.G.A.3, 6.G.A.1
  5. Yes; plant heights vary.

    Look for a collection of data and anticipated variation; identify the population and variable.

    6.SP.A.1
  6. n = 6; minutes; mean 9/2 min

    Sum = 27; mean = 27 ÷ 6 = 9/2.

    6.SP.B.5a, 6.SP.B.5b, 6.SP.B.5c
  7. 8: 1 dot; 9: 2 dots; 10: 2 dots; 11: 1 dot; n = 6

    Each observation contributes one dot; stack repeated values at their number-line position.

    6.SP.B.4
  8. (7, 9, 10, 11, 13)

    Box runs from 9 to 11, median line at 10, whiskers to 7 and 13.

    6.SP.B.4, 6.SP.B.5c
  9. 6: 1 dot; 10: 2 dots; 14: 2 dots; 18: 1 dot; n = 6

    Each observation contributes one dot; stack repeated values at their number-line position.

    6.SP.B.4
  10. Frequencies 0, 3, 4, 3

    Count every value once. Bars touch and have equal width; horizontal axis is minutes, vertical axis is frequency.

    6.SP.B.4
  11. Q1 11, Q3 17; IQR 6 min; range 18 min

    Lower half [5, 11, 11] has median 11; upper half [17, 17, 23] has median 17. Subtract Q1 from Q3.

    6.SP.A.3, 6.SP.B.5c
  12. Mean 13/2 min; MAD 5/2 min

    Mean = 39/6 = 13/2. Absolute deviations: 9/2, 3/2, 3/2, 3/2, 3/2, 9/2. Their sum divided by 6 is 5/2. This is the average distance from the mean.

    6.SP.B.5c
  13. 6 observations; minutes; median 8, IQR 2; mean 8, MAD 5/3. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 8 and half medians give IQR 2. Mean is 48/6; averaging distances gives MAD 5/3. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

    6.SP.B.5d
  14. 6 observations; minutes; median 16, IQR 6; mean 16, MAD 5. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 16 and half medians give IQR 6. Mean is 96/6; averaging distances gives MAD 5. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

    6.SP.B.5d
  15. 6 observations; minutes; median 14, IQR 6; mean 14, MAD 5. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 14 and half medians give IQR 6. Mean is 84/6; averaging distances gives MAD 5. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

    6.SP.A.2, 6.SP.A.3
  16. 6 observations; minutes; median 27/2, IQR 5; mean 27/2, MAD 25/6. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.

    Order the observations. Middle positions give median 27/2 and half medians give IQR 5. Mean is 81/6; averaging distances gives MAD 25/6. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.

    6.SP.B.5a, 6.SP.B.5b, 6.SP.B.5c, 6.SP.B.5d
  17. First median 9; second 13 min; both IQR 2 min.

    Adding 4 shifts every position and both quartiles by 4. The median increases by 4; Q3 - Q1 remains 2.

    6.SP.A.2, 6.SP.B.5d
  18. 13 students; stacks at 0, 1, 2, 3 have 1, 3, 4, 5 dots.

    Place one dot per student, not one per distinct value. Total dots = 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 13. Label the number line books read.

    6.SP.B.4
  19. B, D - What is the reading time in minutes of each of the students?; How does reading time vary among the students?

    Different students can have different reading time. The questions anticipate variation across a collection. The conversion and arithmetic questions have fixed answers.

    6.SP.A.1
  20. A, D - The median is the average of the 3rd and 4th ordered values.; The median is 9 minutes.

    Six ordered observations have middle positions 3 and 4, so median = (8 + 10)/2 = 9.

    6.SP.B.5c
