Day 076Repeated Factors and Exponents

LearnClassworkAnswer keyPDF packet ↗
Standards 6.EE.A.1
LEARNING TARGET

I can write repeated multiplication using a whole-number exponent.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

The base is the repeated factor and the exponent tells how many copies to multiply. For a positive exponent, 4^3 = 4 × 4 × 4, not 4 × 3. Parentheses show when a whole product is the base.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Write 2^4 as repeated multiplication and evaluate.

2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16

The exponent counts 4 factors of 2; multiply all factors.

WORKED EXAMPLE 2

Write 7^4 as repeated multiplication and evaluate. Explain why it differs from 7 × 4.

7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 2401; 7 × 4 = 28

The exponent counts 4 factors of 7; multiply all factors.
