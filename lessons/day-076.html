Standards 6.RP.A.1

enVision Topic 5: Understand and Use Ratios and Rates | Spectrum companion Chapter 3

LEARNING TARGET

I can describe Ratios with Ordered Labels, and explain and check my reasoning.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

A ratio compares two quantities multiplicatively. In a:b, a names the first quantity and b names the second. Divide both terms by a common factor to simplify, while keeping the labels and order. For a:b, the total is a+b equal parts. The first part is a/(a+b) of the total; a/b compares it to the second part. Write labels before choosing the denominator.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

A tray has 3 red tiles and 5 blue tiles. Write the red-to-blue ratio in simplest form.

Step-by-step solution

  1. A ratio compares two quantities multiplicatively.
  2. In a:b, a names the first quantity and b names the second.
  3. Keep red first
  4. divide 3:5 by 1.
Answer: 3:5
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple select

A bag contains 25 red tiles and 30 blue tiles. Select all true statements.

A. The fraction of all tiles that are red is 5/11.

B. The red-to-blue ratio is 5:11.

C. The red-to-blue ratio is 5:6.

D. There are 56 tiles in all.

Step-by-step solution

  1. For a:b, the total is a+b equal parts.
  2. Divide both color counts by 5.
  3. For the fraction of the whole, use total 55 as denominator
  4. red-to-blue compares just the two colors.
Answer: A, C - The fraction of all tiles that are red is 5/11.; The red-to-blue ratio is 5:6.

Answers and reasoning
