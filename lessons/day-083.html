LEARNING TARGET

I can find a unit rate and explain its units.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

A unit rate compares an amount with one unit of another quantity. Divide the first quantity by the second. Write the units, such as dollars per notebook, so you know what the quotient means.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

5 identical notebooks cost $10. Find the cost per notebook.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    A unit rate compares an amount with one unit of another quantity.

  2. Step 2

    Divide the first quantity by the second.

  3. Step 3

    Unit rate = 10 ÷ 5 = 2 dollars per notebook.

Answer: $2 per notebook
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple select

6 notebooks cost $42. Select all statements at the same price per notebook.

A. 5 notebooks cost $36.

B. 5 notebooks cost $35.

C. Each notebook costs $252.

D. Each notebook costs $7.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    A unit rate compares an amount with one unit of another quantity.

  2. Step 2

    Cost per notebook = 42 ÷ 6 = 7.

  3. Step 3

    Multiply $7 by the chosen notebook count.

Answer: B, D - 5 notebooks cost $35.; Each notebook costs $7.
