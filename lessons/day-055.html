enVision Topic 3: Numeric and Algebraic Expressions | Spectrum companion Chapter 5

LEARNING TARGET

I can connect Distribution and Expression Structure, and explain and check my reasoning.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Multiply the factor outside parentheses by every term inside. The two expressions have the same value for every allowed input. An area model can show how one rectangle splits into smaller rectangles. Replace every variable with its assigned value, using parentheses. Apply the order of operations. In formulas, label the answer with the appropriate measurement units.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Expand 6(x + 4).

  1. Multiply the factor outside parentheses by every term inside.
  2. The two expressions have the same value for every allowed input.
  3. Multiply the outside factor by every inside term, including the constant.
  4. State the result in the form requested: 6x + 24.
6x + 24
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Numeric entry and reasoning

Let x = 2 and y = 5. Evaluate 4x + y². Part A: enter the value. Part B: show substitution and the order of operations.

  1. Replace every variable with its assigned value, using parentheses.
  2. Substitute: 4 × (2) + 5².
  3. The product is 8 and the square is 25.
  4. Add to get 33.
33

03 · CHECK

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
  1. 2x + 12

    Multiply the outside factor by every inside term, including the constant.

    6.EE.A.3
  2. 20

    Substitute first: 8 × 2 + 4 = 20.

    6.EE.A.2c
  3. 5x + 10

    Multiply the outside factor by every inside term, including the constant.

    6.EE.A.3
  4. 56

    Substitute first: 4^2 + 8 × 5 = 56.

    6.EE.A.2c
  5. 42x + 21

    Multiply the outside factor by every inside term, including the constant.

    6.EE.A.3
  6. 20

    Substitute first: 4^2 + 2 × 2 = 20.

    6.EE.A.2c
  7. 49x + 42; both give 140 at x = 2

    Multiply the outside factor by every inside term, including the constant.

    6.EE.A.3
  8. 19

    Substitute: 3 × (1) + 4². The product is 3 and the square is 16. Add to get 19.

    6.EE.A.2c
  9. A, C - 6 + 3x; 3x + 6

    Distribute 3 to both terms to get 3x + 6. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

    6.EE.A.3
  10. A = 6(x + 3); 48 square units

    Substitute x = 5: 6(5 + 3) = 48.

    6.EE.A.2c
