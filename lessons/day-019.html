LEARNING TARGET

I can model fraction division as equal-sized groups.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

A quotient asks how many groups of the divisor fit in the dividend. Rename both fractions with a common denominator, draw equal parts, and count groups. A partial group is a fraction of the divisor, not of the original whole.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Draw a bar partitioned into 10 equal parts. Shade 4 parts. Circle groups of 3 parts. Use the model to find 2/5 ÷ 3/10.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Split one whole into 10 equal parts.

    Each part is 1/10 of the whole.
  2. Step 2

    Shade 4 parts. Use 3 shaded parts for each full group.

  3. Step 3

    Divide the count of shaded parts by the group size.

    4 ÷ 3 = 4/3 groups
Answer: 4/3
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple choice

Draw a bar partitioned into 6 equal parts. Shade 4 parts. Circle groups of 2 parts. Use the model to find 2/3 ÷ 1/3. Explain the partial group. Select one answer, then justify it with a model or calculation.

A. 2

B. 20

C. 3

D. 1

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Split one whole into 6 equal parts.

    Each part is 1/6 of the whole.
  2. Step 2

    Shade 4 parts. Use 2 shaded parts for each full group.

  3. Step 3

    Divide the count of shaded parts by the group size.

    4 ÷ 2 = 2 groups
Answer: A - 2
