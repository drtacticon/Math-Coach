Day 127 · Lesson 07Find Quartiles and Interquartile Range

LearnPracticeDaily PDF ↗
Standards 6.SP.A.36.SP.B.5c

enVision Topic 8: Display, Describe, and Summarize Data | Spectrum companion Chapter 7

LEARNING TARGET

I can calculate quartiles and interquartile range.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Sort the data. Find the median, then the median of each half for Q1 and Q3. For an odd count, exclude the overall median from both halves in this course. IQR = Q3 - Q1; range = maximum - minimum.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Reading times in minutes are 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5. Find Q1, Q3, IQR, and range.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Sort the data.

  2. Step 2

    Find the median, then the median of each half for Q1 and Q3.

  3. Step 3

    Lower half [2, 3, 3] has median 3

  4. Step 4

    upper half [4, 4, 5] has median 4.

  5. Step 5

    Subtract Q1 from Q3.

Answer: Q1 3, Q3 4; IQR 1 min; range 3 min
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multi-field numeric entry and explanation

Six students recorded reading times (minutes): 5, 11, 11, 17, 17, 23. Part A: enter Q1 and Q3. Part B: enter IQR and explain which portion of the data it describes.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Sort the data.

  2. Step 2

    Lower-half median is 11

  3. Step 3

    upper-half median is 17.

  4. Step 4

    IQR = 17 - 11 = 6, the span of the middle half of the ordered data.

Answer: Q1 = 11; Q3 = 17; IQR = 6 min

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
