03 · CHECK

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
  1. 28 oaks and 16 pines

    11 equal sections total; each section is 44 ÷ 11 = 4.

  2. 10 square meters; aligned pairs (0,0), (4,5), (8,10)

    8 ÷ 4 = 2 batches; 2 × 5 = 10.

  3. 9 oaks and 6 pines

    5 equal sections total; each section is 15 ÷ 5 = 3.

  4. 3 yards

    9 ÷ 3 = 3; 1 yards = 3 feet.

  5. $4 per notebook

    Unit rate = 16 ÷ 4 = 4 dollars per notebook.

  6. $7 per notebook

    Unit rate = 35 ÷ 5 = 7 dollars per notebook.

  7. 15 km/h

    Speed = 90 ÷ 6 = 15; distance = speed × time.

  8. 3 hours

    Time = distance ÷ speed = 105 ÷ 35 = 3.

  9. A $4/marker; B $4.5/marker; A is cheaper

    Divide each price by its marker count; compare 4 with 4.5.

  10. A $4/marker; B $4.5/marker; A is cheaper

    Divide each price by its marker count; compare 4 with 4.5.

  11. 3/10; 0.3

    30% = 30/100 = 3/10; divide by 100 for 0.3.

  12. 165

    75/100 × 220 = 165.

  13. 12

    5/100 × 240 = 12.

  14. 100

    Whole = 60 ÷ 3/5 = 100. Check 60% × 100 = 60.

  15. 40%

    16 ÷ 40 × 100 = 40%; the reference whole is 40.

  16. 220

    Whole = 44 ÷ 1/5 = 220. Check 20% × 220 = 44.

  17. $3.5 per notebook; $28

    Unit rate = 17.5 ÷ 5 = 3.5 dollars per notebook.

  18. $4 per notebook; $24

    Unit rate = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 dollars per notebook.

  19. 40 km/h; 200 km

    Speed = 40 ÷ 1 = 40; distance = speed × time.

  20. 7/2 hours

    Time = distance ÷ speed = 70 ÷ 20 = 7/2.
