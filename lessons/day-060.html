Unit Test

Begin with previous-unit review, then work through current-unit application and reasoning.

  1. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    A marker is 28 m above sea level. Write its signed elevation and explain what elevation 0 means.

  2. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Find the opposite of -8. Show both values on a number line.

  3. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Draw a horizontal or vertical number line. Plot and label -8, 0, and 7.

  4. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Draw a horizontal or vertical number line. Plot and label -5, 0, and 3.

  5. Constructed response

    Write 4² as repeated multiplication and evaluate.

  6. Constructed response

    Evaluate 5 + 4 × 4. Show the intermediate steps.

  7. Constructed response

    Find the GCF of 10 and 15. Show factor pairs or prime factors.

  8. Constructed response

    Find the LCM of 8 and 9 by listing multiples.

  9. Constructed response

    Find the GCF of 24 and 36. Show factor pairs or prime factors.

  10. Constructed response

    Factor 28 + 42 using its GCF. Make the inside numbers relatively prime.

  11. Constructed response

    For (5x + 7) ÷ 2, name the outer operation, the dividend as a single entity, and the divisor. Identify the sum inside the parentheses.

  12. Expression entry and reasoning

    A student buys x markers at $4 each and y folders at $3 each, then uses one $2 coupon. Part A: enter an expression for the total cost. Part B: explain why the coupon is subtracted only once.

  13. Numeric entry and reasoning

03 · CHECK

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
  1. +28 m; 0 means at sea level

    Sea level is the reference. Below is negative and above is positive.

    6.NS.C.5
  2. 8

    -8 and 8 are each 8 units from 0 on opposite sides.

    6.NS.C.6a
  3. -8 < 0 < 7; unit steps

    Negative -8 is left of or below 0; 7 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

    6.NS.C.6c
  4. -5 < 0 < 3; unit steps

    Negative -5 is left of or below 0; 3 is right of or above 0. Use equal intervals.

    6.NS.C.6c
  5. 4 × 4 = 16

    The exponent counts 2 factors of 4; multiply all factors.

    6.EE.A.1
  6. 21

    Use parentheses and exponents first, then × and ÷ left to right, then + and -. Result: 21.

    6.EE.A.1, 6.EE.A.2c
  7. 5

    Common factors: 1, 5; greatest is 5.

    6.NS.B.4a
  8. 72

    The first positive multiple shared by 8 and 9 is 72.

    6.NS.B.4b
  9. 12

    Common factors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12; greatest is 12.

    6.NS.B.4a
  10. 14(2 + 3)

    GCF = 14; 28 = 14 × 2 and 42 = 14 × 3.

    6.NS.B.4c
  11. Outer quotient; dividend (5x + 7); divisor 2; inner sum 5x + 7

    The grouped sum is one complete quantity being divided. Within it, the terms are 5x and 7.

    6.EE.A.2b
  12. 4x + 3y - 2

    Marker cost is 4x; folder cost is 3y. Add these subtotals, then subtract the one fixed coupon of 2. An expression does not need an equals sign.

    6.EE.A.2a, 6.EE.B.6
  13. 13

    Substitute: 4 × (1) + 3². The product is 4 and the square is 9. Add to get 13.

    6.EE.A.2c
  14. A, D - 42 + 7x; 7x + 42

    Distribute 7 to both terms to get 7x + 42. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

    6.EE.A.3
  15. C, D - 30 + 6x; 6x + 30

    Distribute 6 to both terms to get 6x + 30. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

    6.EE.A.3
  16. A, B - 7x + 4; (2 + 5)x + 4

    Combine coefficients of like x terms: 2 + 5 = 7. The constant 4 stays separate.

    6.EE.A.3
  17. C, D - 4x + 24; 24 + 4x

    Distribute 4 to both terms to get 4x + 24. Reversing the addends gives the same value. The other expressions fail, for example at x = 0.

    6.EE.A.4
  18. 23/2

    Substitute: 5 × (3/2) + 2². The product is 15/2 and the square is 4. Add to get 23/2.

    6.EE.A.2c
  19. B, D - 6 × 6 × 6; 216

    The exponent 3 means three factors of 6; their product is 216. It does not mean 6 times 3.

    6.EE.A.1
  20. 18

    Parentheses: 6 - 3 = 3. Exponent: 3² = 9. Multiply: 3 × 3 = 9. Add: 9 + 9 = 18. The product must be completed before the final sum.

    6.EE.A.1, 6.EE.A.2c
