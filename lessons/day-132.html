LEARNING TARGET

I can write a statistical summary tied to its context.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

A useful summary names the group and attribute, units, observation count, center, variability, and shape. Explain why the chosen center and spread fit the data and mention any striking value.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Reading times in minutes are 8, 9, 9, 10, 10, 11. Write a summary with count, attribute, units, center, variability, shape, and a justified choice of measures.

  1. A useful summary names the group and attribute, units, observation count, center, variability, and shape.
  2. Explain why the chosen center and spread fit the data and mention any striking value.
  3. Calculate the center and spread before interpreting them in context.
  4. Other accurate, justified summaries are acceptable.
  5. State the result in the form requested: 6 observations of reading time in minutes. Mean 19/2 min, MAD 5/6 min; roughly symmetric, so mean/MAD are reasonable..
6 observations of reading time in minutes. Mean 19/2 min, MAD 5/6 min; roughly symmetric, so mean/MAD are reasonable.
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Statistical reasoning and explanation

Six students recorded reading times (minutes): 2, 4, 4, 6, 6, 8. Part A: describe center and spread using appropriate measures. Part B: describe shape, count, units, and any striking value; justify the chosen measures.

  1. A useful summary names the group and attribute, units, observation count, center, variability, and shape.
  2. Order the observations.
  3. Middle positions give median 5 and half medians give IQR 2.
  4. Mean is 30/6
  5. averaging distances gives MAD 5/3.
  6. Tie the chosen summary to the displayed shape and context.
6 observations; minutes; median 5, IQR 2; mean 5, MAD 5/3. Roughly balanced; mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification.
