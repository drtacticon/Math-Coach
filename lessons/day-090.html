02 · ASSESSMENT

Unit Test

Begin with previous-unit review, then work through current-unit application and reasoning.

  1. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Does x = 3 solve 2x + 7 = 11? Show substitution.

  2. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Solve x + 4 = 6. Check in the original equation.

  3. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Solve x - 8 = 5/2. Check in the original equation.

  4. Previous unit review · Constructed response

    Solve 2 × x = 12. Check in the original equation.

  5. Constructed response

    A tray has 7 red tiles and 2 blue tiles. Write the red-to-blue ratio in simplest form.

  6. Constructed response

    A mix uses 6 cups of oats for 3 cups of seeds. Complete the table (oats, seeds): (6, 3), (12, ___), (36, ___).

  7. Constructed response

    Mix A has 2 cups juice for 4 cups water. Mix B has 12 cups juice for 16 cups water. Which is stronger in juice? Show equivalent ratios.

  8. Constructed response

    The ratio of oak trees to pine trees is 6:3. There are 45 trees altogether. Draw a tape diagram and find each count.

  9. Constructed response

    A mix uses 7 cups of oats for 3 cups of seeds. Complete the table (oats, seeds): (7, 3), (14, ___), (14, ___).

  10. Constructed response

    Draw aligned number lines for 3 liters of paint covering 6 square meters. Mark 0, one batch, and 5 batches. How much area do 15 liters cover?

  11. Constructed response

    For every 6 tickets sold, a club earns $3. Make a table for 0, 1, 2, and 5 batches. Plot (tickets, dollars), label axes, and interpret (30, 15).

  12. Multiple select

    3 notebooks cost $18. Select all statements at the same price per notebook.

  13. Two-part numeric entry and reasoning

    Store A sells 5 notebooks for $20. Store B sells 6 notebooks for $30. Part A: enter the unit price at each store. Part B: choose the better buy and explain why package totals alone are insufficient.

  14. Multiple select

  15. Multiple select

03 · CHECK

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
  1. No; left side = 13

    2 × 3 + 7 = 13; compare with 11.

    6.EE.B.5
  2. x = 2

    Subtract 4 on both sides. Then x = 2; substitution gives 6 on each side.

    6.EE.B.7
  3. x = 21/2

    Add 8 on both sides. Then x = 21/2; substitution gives 5/2 on each side.

    6.EE.B.7
  4. x = 6

    Divide by 2 on both sides. Then x = 6; substitution gives 12 on each side.

    6.EE.B.7
  5. 7:2

    Keep red first; divide 7:2 by 1.

    6.RP.A.1
  6. 6; 18

    Multiply both parts by 2 and by 6.

    6.RP.A.3a
  7. Mix B

    For 16 cups water, A uses 8 cups juice; B uses 12. B has more juice per equal water amount.

    6.RP.A.3a
  8. 30 oaks and 15 pines

    9 equal sections total; each section is 45 ÷ 9 = 5.

    6.RP.A.3a
  9. 6; 6

    Multiply both parts by 2 and by 2.

    6.RP.A.3a
  10. 30 square meters; aligned pairs (0,0), (3,6), (15,30)

    15 ÷ 3 = 5 batches; 5 × 6 = 30.

    6.RP.A.3a
  11. (0,0), (6,3), (12,6), (30,15); 30 tickets earns $15

    Scale both quantities by each batch count; plot horizontal tickets and vertical dollars.

    6.RP.A.3a
  12. A, D - 4 notebooks cost $24.; Each notebook costs $6.

    Cost per notebook = 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Multiply $6 by the chosen notebook count.

    6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3b
  13. A: $4 each; B: $5 each; A is the better buy.

    Divide each cost by its own notebook count: 20/5 = 4; 30/6 = 5. Compare the same one-notebook unit.

    6.RP.A.3b
  14. B, C - 2 notebooks cost $4.; Each notebook costs $2.

    Cost per notebook = 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Multiply $2 by the chosen notebook count.

    6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3b
  15. B, D - The speed is 30 km per hour.; In 3 hours it travels 90 km.

    Divide distance by time: 120 ÷ 4 = 30 km/h. Multiply this unit rate by 3 for the later distance.

    6.RP.A.3b
  16. C, D - 30 inches; 5/2 × 12 inches

    There are 12 smaller units in each larger one. Multiply 5/2 by 12, giving 30 inches. The physical length does not change.

    6.RP.A.3d
  17. B, C - 5/2 × 100 centimeters; 250 centimeters

    There are 100 smaller units in each larger one. Multiply 5/2 by 100, giving 250 centimeters. The physical length does not change.

    6.RP.A.3d
  18. A, B - In 5 hours it travels 125 km.; The speed is 25 km per hour.

    Divide distance by time: 75 ÷ 3 = 25 km/h. Multiply this unit rate by 5 for the later distance.

    6.RP.A.3b
  19. B, D - The fraction of all tiles that are red is 2/7.; The red-to-blue ratio is 2:5.

    Divide both color counts by 2. For the fraction of the whole, use total 14 as denominator; red-to-blue compares just the two colors.

    6.RP.A.1
  20. A, C - (15, 6); (10, 4)

    Equivalent ratios multiply both quantities by the same factor. A uses 2; B uses 3. C and D change only one scaled quantity.

    6.RP.A.3a
