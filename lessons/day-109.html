Day 109 · Lesson 04Use Coordinates to Find Polygon Lengths and Area

LearnPracticeDaily PDF ↗
Standards 6.G.A.36.G.A.1

enVision Topic 7: Solve Area, Surface Area, and Volume Problems | Spectrum companion Chapter 6

LEARNING TARGET

I can draw polygons from coordinates and use side lengths to find area.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Plot vertices in order and connect them, including the last to the first. Equal x-values make a vertical side; equal y-values make a horizontal side. Count or subtract coordinates to get the side lengths.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Plot and connect (0,0), (13,0), (13,8), (0,8). Find the side lengths and area.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Plot vertices in order and connect them, including the last to the first.

  2. Step 2

    Equal x-values make a vertical side; equal y-values make a horizontal side.

  3. Step 3

    Horizontal length 13 - (0) = 13

  4. Step 4

    vertical length 8 - (0) = 8

  5. Step 5

    area = 13 × 8.

Answer: Sides 13 and 8; area 104 square units
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Coordinate plotting and two-part response

A rectangle has vertices (-3, -4), (5, -4), (5, 4), and (-3, 4). Plot and connect them. Part A: find its side lengths. Part B: enter its area in square units.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Plot vertices in order and connect them, including the last to the first.

  2. Step 2

    Horizontal length = 3 + 5

  3. Step 3

    vertical length = 4 + 4.

  4. Step 4

    Multiply 8 × 8 = 64.

Answer: 8 by 8 units; area 64 square units

Answers and reasoning

Reveal the teacher answer key
