LEARNING TARGET

I can solve Decimal Measurement Problems, and explain and check my reasoning.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Multiply as whole numbers using the standard algorithm. The product has the total number of decimal places in the two factors before trailing zeros are removed. Check its size against an estimate. Multiply both dividend and divisor by the same power of 10 so the divisor is a whole number. Then use long division, adding zeros to the dividend as needed. Check by multiplication.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Use the standard algorithm: 43 × 8.8. Show your regrouping or division steps.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Count the decimal places in both factors. Multiply as whole numbers first.

    43 × 8.8 → 43 × 88
  2. Step 2

    Multiply by this digit’s place value.

    43 × 8 = 344
  3. Step 3

    Multiply by this digit’s place value.

    43 × 80 = 3440
  4. Step 4

    Add the partial products.

    344 + 3440 = 3784
  5. Step 5

    Place the decimal 1 places from the right.

    43 × 8.8 = 378.4
Answer: 378.4
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Multiple choice

Use the standard algorithm: 1.984 ÷ 0.64. Show your regrouping or division steps. Select one answer, then justify it with a model or calculation.

A. 21/10

B. 31

C. 41/10

D. 31/10

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Multiply both dividend and divisor by the same power of 10 so the divisor is a whole number.

  2. Step 2

    Multiply both numbers by 100.

    198.4 ÷ 64 = 3.1.
  3. Step 3

    Check.

    0.64 × 3.1 = 1.984.
Answer: D - 31/10
