Day 054 · Lesson 09Evaluate Expressions and Formulas

LearnPracticeDaily PDF ↗
Standards 6.EE.A.2c

enVision Topic 3: Numeric and Algebraic Expressions | Spectrum companion Chapter 5

LEARNING TARGET

I can substitute values and evaluate expressions or formulas.

01 · LEARN

Build the idea

Replace every variable with its assigned value, using parentheses. Apply the order of operations. In formulas, label the answer with the appropriate measurement units.

WORKED EXAMPLE 1 · Constructed response

Evaluate 4x + 4 for x = 6.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Replace every variable with its assigned value, using parentheses.
  2. Apply the order of operations.
  3. Substitute first: 4 × 6 + 4 = 28.
Answer: 28
WORKED EXAMPLE 2 · Numeric entry and reasoning

Let x = 5/2 and y = 5. Evaluate 4x + y². Part A: enter the value. Part B: show substitution and the order of operations.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Replace every variable with its assigned value, using parentheses.
  2. Substitute: 4 × (5/2) + 5².
  3. The product is 10 and the square is 25.
  4. Add to get 35.
Answer: 35
